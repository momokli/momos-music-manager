# momo's music manager — user story

multi-service library sync and harmonic discovery for every music or dj software that reads the comment field of your music files.

tag your music by putting it in playlists on spotify or soundcloud, the playlist names will be your tags.

configure your dj environment to display the comment field of your tracks, create smart-filters using the comment field, or just use your library for key-words.

a tag/playlist can be anything, any associated word or sentence (i-prefer-to-keep-sentences-like-so-as-tag-names-but-that-is-preference), which helps you later when digging or playing.
